# GRE 9.2 · Verbal Section 3（填空题部分）

1. Whereas water on Mars could plausibly have been delivered by repeated comet impacts in the geologically recent past, the ice on smaller bodies is more difficult to attribute to ______ source.
   A. an exogenous
   B. a primordial
   C. a solitary
   D. a terrestrial
   E. an irreducible
   > answer: A
   > explanation: Whereas 转折。Mars 水可归因为彗星撞击（exogenous 外部来源），而小天体冰更难归因于 exogenous source。

2. While acknowledging that the actions that the Prime Minister took in order to remain in office were (i)_____, some politicians nevertheless argue that this small amount of (ii)_____ was justified to keep a reforming government in office.
   A. unethical       D. skulduggery
   B. impractical     E. indolence
   C. quixotic        F. incivility
   > answer: A, D
   > explanation: While 让步转折：承认行为 unethical，但这种 skulduggery（诡计）为了保持改革政府是可接受的。

3. In Elizabeth Bishop's prose we can see, even more clearly than in her poetry, some central truths about her nature as a writer. One such truth is that she was no literary critic. Bishop was unable to write about published works—works that were, in other words, always (i)_____, permanently in print, immortal. Rather, she was stirred only by the pathos of what (ii)_____. The ephemerality, pitifulness, and obscurity of things have a central part in Bishop's prose, which strives, almost to excess, to (iii)_____ vanished minutiae.
   A. dubious          D. coalesces       G. test
   B. profound        E. disappears      H. expunge
   C. available       F. evolves         I. chronicle
   > answer: C, E, I
   > explanation: 已出版作品 always available / in print / immortal；Bishop 只被 ephemeral（会消失）事物打动；因此她的散文 strive to chronicle（记录）vanished minutiae。

6. Despite its best efforts to stimulate sales, the bookselling business remains far from ______, for it has high fixed costs in wages and rent, and falling prices make these ever harder to meet.
   A. effective
   B. healthy
   C. innovative
   D. robust
   E. stingy
   F. parsimonious
   > answer: B, D
   > explanation: for 解释：高固定成本 + 价格下跌 → 行业远非 healthy / robust（健康/稳健）。

7. Commentators have been struck by the way in which the politician's drive, _____ the scope and spectacle of her policy proposals, complements one of the principal themes of her campaign, which is the necessity of pursuing grand goals.
   A. attributable to
   B. thwarted by
   C. tantamount to
   D. manifest in
   E. defeated by
   F. evident in
   > answer: D, F
   > explanation: 政治家的 drive 体现在政策提案的宏大 scope 与 spectacle 中，与竞选主题一致；manifest in / evident in = 体现在。

8. The field of urban ecology is in _____ phase: just a decade or two ago the idea of doing natural science in a city was something few researchers ever considered.
   A. an ambiguous
   B. an incipient
   C. a nascent
   D. an obscure
   E. a turbulent
   F. a moribund
   > answer: B, C
   > explanation: 几十年前还很少有人考虑 → 新兴领域；incipient / nascent = 初生的、新兴的。

9. For a long time, painter V. S. Gaitonde's reputation, which barely existed in the West, _____ in his native India, partly because his abstract style was viewed as culturally irrelevant, even un-Indian.
   A. flagged
   B. endured
   C. languished
   D. burgeoned
   E. persevered
   F. metamorphosed
   > answer: A, C
   > explanation: barely existed in the West + culturally irrelevant in India → 声誉低迷；flagged / languished = 衰退、不振。
