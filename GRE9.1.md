# GRE 9.1 · Verbal Section 2（填空题部分）

1. The video game company claims that it is in the entertainment business, which implies accessibility and ease but also suggests ______, and thus does a disservice to those who play video games: you take in entertainment but you actively participate in play.
   A. luxury
   B. pleasure
   C. banality
   D. passivity
   E. shoddiness
   > answer: D
   > explanation: 冒号解释：entertainment = passive consumption, play = active participation. 因此空格要填 passivity，与 actively participate 形成对照。

2. Because scientific breakthroughs tend to occur when a prevailing theory's predictions start to fail, scientists sometimes find it (i)______ when a theory persists—as the standard model of particle physics has done—in making (ii)______ predictions.
   A. productive    D. bold
   B. frustrating   E. abstract
   C. surprising    F. correct
   > answer: B, F
   > explanation: Because 因果取同：breakthrough 发生在预测失败时，所以一个理论持续做出正确预测会阻碍 breakthrough，令人 frustrating。

3. The author of the study of the world's best-selling but critically disparaged painter would no doubt say that the size of the artist's reputation (i)______ the book on sociological grounds, and the author would also no doubt argue that critics are wrong to (ii)______ middlebrow taste. But there is a large dose of reverse snobbery threaded through the book, with its repeated claim that the painter's schmaltzy popular works are (iii)______ the masterpieces in museums.
   A. precludes         D. mimic           G. rarely inspired by
   B. diminishes        E. overlook        H. mostly inferior to
   C. justifies         F. countenance     I. often the equal of
   > answer: C, E, I
   > explanation: Best-selling 的声誉 justify 社会学研究；reverse snobbery 认为流行作品 often the equal of 博物馆杰作；因此批评家 wrong to overlook（忽视/鄙视）middlebrow taste。

6. In the eighteenth and nineteenth centuries, Native American nations were hardly ______ entities: they waxed and waned, with some gaining power and others losing it.
   A. authoritarian
   B. static
   C. cohesive
   D. fragile
   E. unvarying
   F. despotic
   > answer: B, E
   > explanation: hardly + 冒号解释：waxed and waned = 变化，因此不是 static / unvarying。

7. ______ the idea that attention is a limited resource, scientists have repeatedly observed that drivers using mobile phones are slower to react and more apt to miss important details than are drivers focused solely on the road.
   A. Extending
   B. Bolstering
   C. Obscuring
   D. Buttressing
   E. Eclipsing
   F. Complicating
   > answer: B, D
   > explanation: 后面 scientists have repeatedly observed 提供证据支持前面 idea，因此选 bolstering / buttressing（支持）。

8. Epidemiological studies have found that occasional tea drinkers, and those who avoid tea altogether, do not reap the benefits of lowered risk of heart attack that those who ______ the brew do.
   A. eschew
   B. appreciate
   C. sip
   D. forswear
   E. swill
   F. guzzle
   > answer: E, F
   > explanation: 与 occasional / avoid 形成对比，能获 benefits 的是大量饮用者，因此 swill / guzzle（豪饮）为同义对。

9. Using elaborate costumes and lighting, dancer Loïe Fuller transformed herself multiple times during a performance, thereby emphasizing the _____ of character.
   A. fluidity
   B. honesty
   C. universality
   D. sincerity
   E. complexity
   F. mutability
   > answer: A, F
   > explanation: transformed herself multiple times = 变化，因此 fluidity / mutability 为同义对。
